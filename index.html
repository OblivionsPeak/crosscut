How this works

Crosscut pulls public RSS feeds from 50+ US outlets, groups articles covering the same story, and shows how that coverage is spread across the political spectrum. A blindspot is a story one side is largely not reporting.

About the lean ratings. These are a hand-curated placement of each outlet on a US left/right axis, based on commonly cited positions in public media-bias work. They are not licensed ratings from AllSides, Ad Fontes or Media Bias/Fact Check — the three services Ground News averages. Ratings apply to the outlet, never to an individual article. Treat them as a rough guide and argue with them: the whole list lives in feeds.json.

Grouping is automatic and imperfect. Stories are clustered by headline similarity. Occasionally coverage of one event lands in two groups, which can make a side look absent when it isn't. Always click through before concluding a blindspot is real.

Headlines and links only — full article text is never copied. Everything links back to the publisher.
